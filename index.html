A Family Legacy

Our Story

In the autumn of 1987, Lucia and Marco Ferretti left their village in the Emilia-Romagna countryside carrying nothing but a leather-bound recipe book and an unshakeable belief that honest food — cooked slowly and served with love — could build a home anywhere in the world.

They opened La Bella Cucina in a narrow street near the Navigli canals of Milan. Word spread quickly. The Bolognese simmered for six hours. The pasta was rolled at dawn. The wine was chosen by Marco himself every Saturday morning at the market. Within a year, the tiny trattoria was full every evening.

Today, their daughter Sofia and son Alessandro carry the tradition forward — honouring every recipe while weaving in inspiration from the seasons and from their own travels through Puglia, Sicily, and beyond. The leather recipe book still lives in the kitchen, its pages stained with decades of olive oil and memory.
